Mental wellbeing

What it means and why it matters

Mental wellbeing means having enough steadiness to handle everyday stress, notice how you feel, and respond in a way that supports you. It is not about being perfect or happy all the time. It is about giving yourself enough care to keep moving through life with a little more balance and ease.

A few small habits can make a difference: pausing to name your mood, writing down what is on your mind, and choosing one kind action for yourself.

MoodBloom is designed to help with those small moments. You can check in quickly, write more when you need it, and use the anonymous space when you want a lighter, more open place to share.

How to use

A simple path through the app

1. Check in Choose a mood and add a note.

Use this when you want a quick read on your day and a supportive reply that matches what you’re feeling. Track your progress over time.

2. Reflect Write more when you need space.

Open the journal for longer thoughts, private reflections, or anything you want to keep for later. You can also review your previous entries to see your growth over time.

3. Connect Share anonymously or browse gently.

Visit the heart dump to post a thought, your feelings or your experiences without your name attached, or read supportive posts and various experiences shared by others.
